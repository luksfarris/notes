# Polish Verbs

Verbs in Polish may have three tenses: past, present, and future. While most verbs have 
present and past conjugations, I think only the verb to be has a dedicated future tense.

Verbs also vary according to the person, number, and pronoun. Depending if they describe the first,
second, or third person, singular or plural, male or female, the verb will change accordingly.

See the verb to be as an example:

**Verb To Be (Być)**

| Pronoun    | Present   | Past [m/ż]  | Future    |
| ---------- | --------- | ----------- | --------- |
| Ja         | jestem    | byłem/byłam | będę      |
| Ty         | jesteś    | byłeś/byłaś | będziesz  |
| On/Ona/Ono | jest      | był/była    | będzie    |
| My         | jesteśmy  | byliśmy     | będziemy  |
| Wy         | jesteście | byliście    | będziecie |
| Oni/One    | są        | byli        | będą      |

Verbs can also have two aspects, a perfective (dokonany) and an imperfective (niedokonany) mode.
One implies the action will be completed, and the other doesn't.

Verbs also may also have an imperative form (tryb rozkazujący).

## Past Tense (Czas Przeszły)

Verbs in Polish have dedicated conjugations for the past tense. You have to learn them.


## Future Tense (Czas Przyszły)

There are 3 ways to speak about the future, either it's:

1. you will do an action in the future to completion (dokonany/finished)
2. you will do something but don't know if it will be completed, or you know that it will not be completed (niedokonany/unfinished)
3. with an auxiliary verb (future tense of "to be" + past tense of the 3rd person of auxiliary verb + infinitive of the verb or its finished form)

In the case of *dokonany* we simply use the present tense of the verb, and in the case of niedokonany we use the *will* verb. Examples:

- Jutro będe malować obraz. (Tomorrow I will paint a painting - it's implied that I will not complete it)
- Jutro namaluję obraz. (Tomorrow I will paint a painting - it's implied that I will complete it)
- Jutro będę musiał malować obraz. (Tomorrow I will have to paint a painting - it's implied that I will not complete it)
- Jutro będę musiał namalować obraz. (Tomorrow I will have to paint a painting - it's implied that I will complete it)
